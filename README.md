## Guides/FAQ
### Installation
First go to the releases and download the latest release, after downloading click on it and press install. While installing it will can say "Harmful app blocked".

![image alt](https://github.com/MewsterCat/TermLauncher/blob/d55919b75ed555e977ab62a575722ff960a850ef/images/TermLauncher-Installation-Warning-Pic.jpg)

Above a button "Got it" press a text that says "More details ↓". After you clicked it click a text that says "Install anyway".

![image alt](https://github.com/MewsterCat/TermLauncher/blob/d55919b75ed555e977ab62a575722ff960a850ef/images/TermLauncher-Installation-Warning-Pic1.jpg)

### Updating
You don't have to uninstall the already installed one! To update the app you have to download the latest release, when downloaded click on it, it will ask you to update. While updating it will say "Harmful app blocked", above a button "Got it" press a text that says "More details ↓". After you clicked it click a text that says "Install anyway".
