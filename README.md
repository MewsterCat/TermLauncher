## Guides/FAQ
### Installation
While installing the app it can say "harmful app".

![image alt](https://github.com/MewsterCat/TermLauncher/blob/d55919b75ed555e977ab62a575722ff960a850ef/images/TermLauncher-Installation-Warning-Pic.jpg)

Don't worry about that press "more" and click "install anyway".

![image alt](https://github.com/MewsterCat/TermLauncher/blob/d55919b75ed555e977ab62a575722ff960a850ef/images/TermLauncher-Installation-Warning-Pic1.jpg)

### Updating
You don't have to uninstall the already installed one! To update the app you have to download the newest release, when downloaded click on it, it will ask you to update. While updating it will say "Harmful app blocked", above a button "Got it" press a text that says "More details ↓". After you pressed it click a text that says "Install anyway".
